ARM-01 — Robotic Arm Project


Built a 6-servo robotic arm from the ground up using an Arduino Uno and PCA9685 PWM servo driver. Designed in CAD and 3D printed, the arm combines mechanical design, electronics, and real-time software to execute precise assistive tasks in any workspace.

Technical Implementation:

· 6 servo motors mapped across PCA9685 channels (mix of standard and 360° continuous rotation)

· PWM/angle conversion with power-controlled smooth motion interpolation

· Serial command parsing interface for real-time arm control

· Forward kinematics-based collision boundary checking

· Homing routines and claw angle hard limits for safe operation

· Programmed demo sequences showcasing full range of motion


Results:

Successfully demonstrated precise multi-axis control with smooth, power-managed movement. ARM-01 was presented at the ECE Design Fair and recognized for its hardware complexity and build quality among 100 students (teams of 3).
